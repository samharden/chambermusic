## What I did

I accepted Eb, and turned the texture inside out. For the first time the
piano moves continuously: flowing eighths through Eb–Ab–Eb–Cm–Bb7–Eb–Ab–Bb7,
one chord a bar, p, with pedal, the bass walking in halves. Every bar's
figure climbs to the chord's fifth and turns a whole tone above it — C over
Bb at the top of the Eb bars — so the piano's sixth is now simply the tune's
lilt. Violin II's Ab4 from bar 32 becomes a 4–3 suspension over the new
tonic and resolves to G in bar 33. Then the strings, who have spoken only in
fragments for thirty-two bars, enter one by one with long lines: the opening
gesture augmented to 6–2–4–4, in canon at two bars — Violin I from Bb4 in
bar 35, viola from Eb4 in bar 37, cello from Eb3 in bar 39 — each with a
whole tone at the top (Bb–F–G–F, Eb–Bb–C–Bb) because in Eb that is the
diatonic note. The strings are singing the piano's version. Violin II adds a
rising counterline Bb–C–D–Eb in bars 39–40 against the viola's descent. The
cello never gets its descent: at bar 41 the piano cuts everything off, alone
and f, in octaves — Eb–Bb–C–Bb at the original speed, then Ab–G–F falling
not to Eb but to D over Bb2, its own dominant, and stops.

Piano role: **leading** throughout, supporting-by-motion in 33–40 (the
strings are the foreground at mp over its p), soloing in 41–42. Pedal is
intended from 33 to 40; none in 41–42. All string writing is single-line,
longest note one bar. The piano's octaves in 41–42 are single notes per
hand.

## Why

You asked for a different leader and a different rate of motion, and B is
supposed to redistribute the texture. The clearest inversion available was
the one the piece had never done: piano continuous, strings sustained. I
was wary of the brief's warning about strings as a pad, so the long notes
are a canon with staggered entries rather than a chord, and each player has
a bar of rest before entering. The other reason for Eb is that it settles
the sixth without anyone conceding: above Bb, the whole tone is the key's
own note. The piano does not have to argue for C in Eb; it just plays it,
and the strings find themselves doing the same. That felt like the right
consequence of your bar 27 concession — the piano yielded on Ab in C, and
B rewards it. Bars 41–42 are there so B has a first climax and so the
piano's leading role is stated, not just implied by texture.
